About

A practice of listening, inquiry and story.

Bashayar works at the meeting point of psychology and the humanities — grounded in counselling and research, and drawing equally on literature and film to understand how people make meaning of their lives.

Disciplines

Six threads, one practice.

Psychology

Understanding behaviour, emotion and wellbeing through a careful, evidence-based lens.

Counselling

Person-centred support that meets people where they are, with warmth and confidentiality.

Research

Qualitative and applied inquiry into mental health, identity and community life.

Literature

Essays and fiction exploring interior lives, belonging and the quiet dignity of ordinary people.

Film

Visual storytelling and criticism — reading cinema as a mirror of the human condition.

Community

Participation in initiatives that open up honest, stigma-free conversations about wellbeing.
