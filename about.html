About

A practice of listening, inquiry and story.

Psychology and the humanities, held together by respect for the person before the label.

Bashayar works at the meeting point of psychology and the humanities. Grounded in counselling and psychological research, Bashayar draws equally on literature and film to understand how people make meaning of their lives.

Across every discipline the approach is the same: careful listening, evidence-based thinking, and respect for the person before the label. Alongside professional work, Bashayar takes part in community initiatives that make wellbeing conversations more accessible.

The guiding idea is simple: you don't have to be something in order to be something. Human worth does not depend on titles, output or productivity — and good practice, in the counselling room or on the page, starts from there.

Approach

Principles that guide the work.

01

Dignity first

Every person is worth listening to, independent of status, title or achievement.

02

Evidence-based

Claims are grounded in research and stated with care, not hyperbole.

03

Story as understanding

Literature and film are treated as serious ways of knowing what it is to be human.

04

Community

Wellbeing grows in shared, stigma-free spaces, not only in clinics.

Disciplines

Six threads, one practice.

Psychology

Understanding behaviour, emotion and wellbeing through a careful, evidence-based lens.

Counselling

Person-centred support that meets people where they are, with warmth and confidentiality.

Research

Qualitative and applied inquiry into mental health, identity and community life.

Literature

Essays and fiction exploring interior lives, belonging and the quiet dignity of ordinary people.

Film

Visual storytelling and criticism — reading cinema as a mirror of the human condition.

Community

Participation in initiatives that open up honest, stigma-free conversations about wellbeing.
